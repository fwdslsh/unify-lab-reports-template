# Welcome to your lab reports

This is an example article, not a report about a real lab.

Write articles in `site/articles/`, investigations in `site/incidents/`, and
weekly reviews in `site/health/`. Give every page a title and description.
Incidents and weekly reviews also need an actual ISO observation date.

Optional `tags`, `series`, and `part` metadata drive navigation. Unify parses
the metadata and builds the searchable page list; no separate index is maintained.

The dashboard is the homepage. Reports lists investigations and weekly reviews.
Guides contains operational documentation. All pages searches the site's content.
